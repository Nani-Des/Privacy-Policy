Privacy Policy for SOCIALHUBB

Last Updated: April 25, 2025

SocialHubb is a mobile platform designed to help lawyers and clients communicate securely, schedule consultations, and share documents. Protecting your privacy and keeping your information safe is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our services.

By using SocialHubb, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information only as needed to provide our services:

Personal Information

When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and preferred language.

When you schedule a consultation, we collect details such as date, time, and lawyer information.

Messages, documents, or notes you choose to share with your lawyer are stored securely.

Device & Usage Information

With your permission, we may use your camera and microphone for video calls and voice notes. These are processed in real time and not stored without your consent.

We collect basic app usage data (e.g., crash reports, performance logs) to improve reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Provide secure communication between you and your lawyer.

Manage consultation bookings and reminders.

Support document sharing and storage.

Improve app performance and security.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We only share it in the following cases:

With Your Lawyer: Information you send (messages, documents, bookings) is shared only with the lawyer(s) you choose.

With Service Providers: We may use trusted third-party services (e.g., for authentication, video calls, or secure storage). They must protect your data and cannot use it for other purposes.

Legal Reasons: If required by law, or to protect safety and prevent fraud.

4. Data Security

We take strong measures to protect your data:

End-to-end encryption for messages and calls.

Secure storage for documents.

Access restricted to you and your lawyer.

Regular security checks and updates.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data:

You can update or delete your account at any time.

You can revoke app permissions (camera, microphone, notifications) from your device settings.

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by contacting us.

6. Data Retention

Your account and consultation records are kept as long as your account is active.

Messages and documents remain available until you or your lawyer delete them.

Anonymized usage data (e.g., crash reports) may be stored to improve the app.

7. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside your country. We ensure they follow strong data protection laws.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if our services or legal obligations change. We will notify you in the app or by email.
